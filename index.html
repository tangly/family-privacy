Privacy policy of the Family app

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Family is an app to organise a family: tasks, shopping lists, staples, home papers and the medicines at home. This page explains which personal data the app uses, why, who receives it, and what your rights are.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Stephane Carbonnier (Keapps), Alicante, Spain.
Contact: stephane.keapp@gmail.com

2. The data we use

  • Account: your email address, your name and your password. The password is stored in encrypted form only.
  • Family data: the families you create or join, your role, and what you or your family add: tasks, shopping lists and items, staples, home papers and their key dates, and medicines with their expiry date and the people who take them.
  • Voice commands: speech recognition runs on your phone. The audio never leaves your phone. If your family has a subscription, the recognised text is sent to our server to find the task or the shopping items in it. The server does not store or log this text.
  • Notifications: a notification token for each phone where you are signed in, its platform and its language, and your reminder settings.
  • Crash reports: when the app crashes, a report with the error, the app version, the phone model, the operating system version and a random installation identifier.

The app has no advertising and no analytics. We do not sell your data. The app does not read your location or your contacts.

3. Why we use it (legal basis)

  • To provide the app (performance of the contract, GDPR art. 6(1)(b)): your account, your families, sharing with your family, voice commands and reminders.
  • Medicines can reveal health information. You add them yourself, by choice, and they are visible only to your family. We process them on the basis of your explicit consent (GDPR art. 9(2)(a)). You can delete them at any time.
  • Notifications and microphone are used only after you allow them on your phone. You can turn them off in the phone settings.
  • Crash reports (legitimate interest, GDPR art. 6(1)(f)): to find and fix errors in the app.

4. Who receives your data

  • Your family: the members of a family see your name, your email address, your role and what is shared in that family.
  • Supabase hosts the accounts and the database, in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Google Firebase delivers notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and collects crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Anthropic receives the text of a voice command, with the first names of your family members, only when your family has a subscription. It returns the task or the items found in the text. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this text to train its models.

These companies process the data only on our behalf. Some of them can process it outside the European Union, for example in the United States. In that case the transfer is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account and your family data: until you delete them, or until you delete your account.
  • Finished tasks: deleted automatically about 30 days after their date.
  • Shopping history (used to suggest items): up to 180 days.
  • Voice command counters (used for limits): 2 days.
  • Notification tokens: until you sign out on that phone or delete your account.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days, at Firebase.

6. Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings → Delete my account. This deletes your account, your email address and your name, your notification tokens and your reminder settings. You leave all your families. A family where you were the only member is deleted. What you added to a family that has other members stays in that family, but it is no longer linked to you.

If you cannot open the app, send an email to stephane.keapp@gmail.com from the address of your account and we delete it for you.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, to limit its use, or object to it. You can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to stephane.keapp@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

8. Children

A child under 14 must not create an account without the consent of a parent or guardian.

9. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). In the database, each family can read only its own data.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date. For an important change, we also tell you in the app.
